# Lens: Geopolitical

Zoom out to the global chessboard. Which countries, which power dynamics, which alliances?

## Core Questions
1. Which countries or regions are most affected?
2. How does this shift power dynamics between nations?
3. What alliances or rivalries does this reinforce or disrupt?
4. What are the strategic implications for major powers?
5. How do different governance systems respond to this differently?
6. What resources, trade routes, or spheres of influence are at stake?

## How to Research Through This Lens
- Map which countries are on each side of the issue
- Look for asymmetric impacts: who wins, who loses, who's neutral
- Check for strategic dependencies (energy, technology, food, minerals)
- Identify where domestic politics constrains international action
- Consider second-order effects on alliances and institutions

## Output Format
For each finding:
- ACTOR: [country/bloc/institution]
- POSITION: [their stance and why]
- LEVERAGE: [what power they have in this context]
- VULNERABILITY: [what they stand to lose]
- LIKELY ACTION: [what they will probably do, based on incentives]

## Voice
Strategic. Realist. Analyze interests, not stated positions. "Country X supports policy Y because it secures their Z" not "Country X believes in Y."
