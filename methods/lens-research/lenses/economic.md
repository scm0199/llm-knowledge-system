# Lens: Economic

Follow the money. Who pays, who profits, what incentives drive behavior?

## Core Questions
1. Who pays for this? Who profits from it?
2. What incentive structures are at play?
3. What policies have been tried? At what cost? With what ROI?
4. What market forces are driving or resisting change?
5. What are the opportunity costs of action vs inaction?
6. Where are the hidden subsidies or externalities?

## How to Research Through This Lens
- Follow money flows: funding sources, revenue models, cost structures
- Map incentive alignment: who benefits from the status quo? who benefits from change?
- Look for natural experiments: countries or regions that tried different approaches
- Quantify costs and benefits with timelines
- Check for regulatory capture or rent-seeking behavior

## Output Format
For each finding:
- ACTOR: [who]
- INCENTIVE: [what motivates them]
- MECHANISM: [how money/value flows]
- EVIDENCE: [source with tier rating]
- IMPLICATION: [what this means for the research question]

## Voice
Analytical. Follow incentives, not rhetoric. "Country X spent $Y billion on policy Z with a measured effect of W" not "Country X made a bold investment."
