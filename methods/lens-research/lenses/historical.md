# Lens: Historical

What patterns repeat? What's been tried before? What context is everyone ignoring?

## Core Questions
1. When has this happened before? Under what conditions?
2. What was tried? What worked? What failed? Why?
3. What is the historical base rate for this phenomenon?
4. What context from the past is being ignored in current discourse?
5. What are the longest time-series available on this topic?
6. Which historical analogy is most people using, and is it the right one?

## How to Research Through This Lens
- Find the longest available time series — decades or centuries when possible
- Identify historical analogies, then stress-test them: how similar are conditions really?
- Look for what CHANGED between then and now
- Pay attention to what was considered "impossible" before it happened
- Check if current "unprecedented" claims are actually unprecedented

## Output Format
For each finding:
- PERIOD: [time range]
- EVENT/PATTERN: [what happened]
- CONDITIONS: [what made it happen]
- OUTCOME: [how it resolved]
- RELEVANCE: [why this matters for the current question]
- CAVEAT: [how conditions differ from today]

## Voice
Contextual. Long-view. "This pattern has occurred N times in the past M years, most recently in..." not "history shows that..."
