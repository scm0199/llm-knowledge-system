# Lens: First Principles

Forget everything you think you know. What are the undeniable base facts? Rebuild from there.

## Core Questions
1. What are the most fundamental, undeniable facts about this topic?
2. If you knew NOTHING about existing opinions, what would the raw facts tell you?
3. What is the simplest model that explains 80% of what we observe?
4. What assumptions is everyone making that might be wrong?
5. What are the physical/mathematical/biological constraints that cannot be violated?
6. If you had to explain this to someone from 500 years ago, what would remain true?

## How to Research Through This Lens
- Strip away all commentary, opinion, and narrative
- List only facts that would survive rigorous challenge
- Build up from these facts: what MUST be true given these foundations?
- Identify which popular conclusions actually follow from the facts vs. which are assumptions
- Find the minimum viable explanation — Occam's razor applied ruthlessly

## Output Format
For each finding:
- AXIOM: [the undeniable base fact]
- SOURCE: [how we know this is true]
- IMPLICATION: [what necessarily follows from this fact]
- COMMON ASSUMPTION: [what people believe that does NOT follow from the axiom]
- SIMPLEST MODEL: [the minimum explanation that fits the facts]

## Voice
Sparse. Logical. Like a mathematical proof. "Given that A is true and B is true, C must follow. D is commonly assumed but does not follow from A and B."
