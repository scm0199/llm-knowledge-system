# Socratic Review: [Topic]

## Purpose

This review applies five categories of Socratic questioning to every CONSENSUS-grade, DISPUTED, and key UNVERIFIED finding from the consensus matrix. The goal is **not** to disprove — it's to surface what the evidence quietly assumes, what perspective is missing, and what follows from these claims being true.

## Questioning categories

| Category | Core questions |
|---|---|
| **Clarification** | What exactly do we mean by [term]? Is the scope universal or conditional? Is there ambiguity across sources? |
| **Assumption** | What are we assuming to accept this? Is that assumption justified? What changes if it's false? |
| **Evidence** | How strong is the evidence? Methodology? Bias risk? Recency? |
| **Perspective** | Who benefits from this being true? Whose view is missing? |
| **Implication** | If true, what necessarily follows? Second-order effects? Contradictions with other findings? |
